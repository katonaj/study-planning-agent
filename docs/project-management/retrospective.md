# Sprint Retrospective - Week 8 (Simulated)
 
## What went well?
- A single outcome-based Sprint Goal linked PB-01, PB-02 and PB-03.
- A visible board prevented unfinished T-07 and T-08 from being called Done.
- Team members discussed domain-versus-export responsibility early enough to document a decision.
 
## What did not go well?
- Missing-deadline acceptance conditions were ambiguous when the simulation started.
- Demo preparation and review capacity were estimated too optimistically.
- Work was initially estimated before checking architectural dependencies.
 
## Root cause
Backlog refinement was too brief: each selected PBI lacked a confirmed example, owner for follow-up questions, and one testable acceptance check.
 
## One specific process improvement
**Before selecting a PBI for the next Sprint, spend 10 minutes on a refinement checklist:**
1. Identify the user value and one example input/output.
2. Write at least one testable acceptance condition.
3. Identify one dependency/risk and assign a contact person.
4. Confirm that the estimate still makes sense.
 
**Owner:** Scrum Master facilitates; Product Owner and Developers participate.  
**When:** before the next Sprint Planning.  
**Success measure:** 100% of selected PBIs have at least one agreed acceptance example and documented dependency before task breakdown.

## Small XP/TDD experiment
Pair on PB-02's highest-risk validation scenario and first describe a failing test (Red), the minimal satisfying behavior (Green), then a clean boundary (Refactor) **in a later implementation Sprint**.
 
## Follow-up at the next retrospective
Check whether acceptance questions were resolved before work began and whether unfinished review tasks decreased. This is about **team process**, whereas the Sprint Review inspected the product direction/outcome.
